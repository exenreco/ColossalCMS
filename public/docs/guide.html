A little guidance. A lot of possibility.

Your quick guide to writing, publishing, and making Colossal your own.

Start with a page or a post

Pages are the lasting parts of your site, like About. Posts are your stories and updates. Choose either in the sidebar, then Create. Add a title, a unique URL slug, an excerpt, and your content. Use the rich-text toolbar for headings, lists, links and media. The right panels contain status, featured image, taxonomy, SEO and revision history.

Publish when you’re ready

Save a Draft to keep work private. Choose Published to release immediately, or Scheduled to select a future date and time in your local timezone. Only published and due scheduled content appears on your site.

Your media library

Upload images, audio and video in Media. Images need alt text. Search, edit metadata, and replace a file while keeping its references. In-use files cannot be deleted. The same library opens from the editor and site icon settings.

Make the site yours

In Settings, change the site title, tagline, accent color, or logo URL. Choose a site icon from Media, and choose whether posts appear on the homepage or a published page. Save, then open your site to see the result. Plugins lets you activate extra features. Locked core plugins always remain active. ZIP uploads are validated and stored inactive; uploaded plugin execution is currently disabled pending approval.

Design with themes

Open Themes in the System group to install a theme ZIP or create a new theme. Edit layouts with the block library, nested outline, and settings inspector. Shared headers and footers update every template. Preview Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile sizes. Save a draft privately, then Publish when ready. More includes clone, ZIP export, version history and revert. Signed site previews last 15 minutes.

Use the content editor’s Template panel to give an individual page or post its own layout. Switching themes preserves all content and warns when an assigned template needs a fallback.

Bring your team

Administrators can add members by email under Team & roles. Editors manage content; administrators also manage the workspace. Site sharing is a separate platform setting. Adding a member does not send an invitation.

Connect an integration

Create a read-only API key in Settings. Copy it when shown, then send it as a Bearer token to /api/content. Private Site access still applies. Revoke unused keys.
